Model Overview

This simulator combines Lotka-Volterra population dynamics with mechanism design constraints to model the AI-web content ecosystem. The model has four state variables that evolve over time, with regime switching when incentive constraints are violated.

1. Core Dynamics (v3: One Mechanism Per Layer)

The model follows a strict causal chain where each mechanism appears exactly once: A→W→πc→C→Q→A. All state variables bounded in [0,1] by construction.

Creator Revenue (πc)

$$\pi_c = \underbrace{\theta \cdot p_L \cdot A}_{\text{AI licensing}} + \underbrace{p_{ad} \cdot W}_{\text{ad revenue}} + \underbrace{p_{sub} \cdot Q}_{\text{subscriptions}}$$
Interpretation: Creator revenue has three channels: licensing payments from AI platforms (scales with θ and A), advertising revenue (proportional to traffic W), and subscription/patronage (scales with quality Q).
θ = compensation rate pL = licensing price pad = ad revenue coefficient psub = subscription coefficient

Creator Dynamics (dC/dt)

$$\frac{dC}{dt} = \underbrace{(1-C) \cdot \rho_C \cdot S(\pi_c - \bar{u})}_{\text{entry when profitable}} - \underbrace{C \cdot \rho_C \cdot S(\bar{u} - \pi_c)}_{\text{exit when unprofitable}}$$
Interpretation: Creators enter when πc > ū (outside option), exit when unprofitable. The (1-C) and C terms ensure C ∈ [0,1] by construction. IR constraint emerges from dynamics.
ρC = adjustment speed S(x) = sigmoid response ū = outside option

Traffic Dynamics (dW/dt)

$$\frac{dW}{dt} = \underbrace{(1-W) \cdot \rho_W \cdot (1-D)}_{\text{traffic recovery}} - \underbrace{W \cdot \rho_W \cdot D}_{\text{AI diversion}}$$
Interpretation: W = traffic to original content. Diversion propensity D(A,θ) = σ(kA(A-a₀) - kθ(θ-θ₀)) increases with AI scale, decreases with compensation. W ∈ [0,1] by construction.
ρW = traffic adjustment speed D = diversion propensity kA, kθ = sensitivity parameters

Quality Dynamics (dQ/dt)

$$\frac{dQ}{dt} = (1-Q)\Big[\underbrace{\nu R(\pi_c)}_{\text{reinvestment}} + \underbrace{\epsilon \max(C-C_{crit},0)}_{\text{critical mass}}\Big] - Q\Big[\underbrace{\lambda_0}_{\text{decay}} + \underbrace{\epsilon \max(C_{crit}-C,0)}_{\text{shortfall}}\Big]$$
Interpretation: Quality improves with reinvestment R(πc) = softplus(πc - π̄) and creator mass above Ccrit. Decays at baseline rate, faster if C < Ccrit. Q ∈ [0,1] by construction.
ν = reinvestment rate ε = critical mass sensitivity Ccrit = critical mass threshold λ₀ = baseline decay

AI Platform Dynamics (dA/dt)

$$\frac{dA}{dt} = \underbrace{(1-A) \cdot \Big( I + r_A A (1 + \gamma Q) \Big)}_{\text{growth (quality-amplified)}} - \underbrace{A \cdot \delta_A}_{\text{depreciation}}$$
Interpretation: AI grows from external investment I plus endogenous growth amplified by quality Q. Low Q throttles endogenous growth but not investment. A ∈ [0,1] by construction.
I = external investment rA = growth rate γ = data advantage strength δA = depreciation rate

2. Mechanism Design Constraints

IR and IC are now emergent properties of the dynamics rather than separate regime switches.

Individual Rationality (IR)

$$\text{IR}: \quad \pi_c \geq \bar{u}$$
Participation constraint: Creators participate when expected revenue exceeds their outside option. Enforced by entry/exit dynamics: when πc < ū, exit rate exceeds entry rate, and C declines naturally.
When violated: Exit dominates entry → C declines → Q falls → feedback loop

Incentive Compatibility (IC)

$$\text{IC}: \quad p_{sub} \cdot \Delta Q \geq \Delta c$$
Quality incentive: High-quality content is incentive compatible when the subscription/patronage premium exceeds additional costs Δc (research, fact-checking, editing). Under [0,1] normalization, ΔQ = 1 so IC simplifies to psub ≥ Δc.
When violated: Low psub → Q contributes less to πc → R(πc) falls → quality improvement slows

3. Critical Thresholds & Equilibrium Structure

Traffic Equilibrium

$$W^* = 1 - D(A^*, \theta)$$
Steady state: Traffic share equals one minus diversion propensity. Higher AI scale or lower compensation → lower W*.

Creator Equilibrium

$$C^* = S(\pi_c^* - \bar{u})$$
Steady state: Creator participation equals sigmoid of profitability vs outside option. When πc* > ū, majority participate; when πc* < ū, majority exit.

AI Equilibrium (First-Order)

$$A^* \approx \frac{I}{\delta_A} \quad \text{(small-}A\text{ approx.)}$$
Steady state: In small-A regime, AI scale ≈ investment/depreciation. For larger A, quality materially affects A* through the endogenous growth term.

Critical Mass Threshold

$$C_{crit}: \text{quality gains/losses hinge on } C \gtrless C_{crit}$$
Tipping point: Above Ccrit, quality has tailwinds (ecosystem thrives). Below Ccrit, quality has headwinds (decline accelerates). Creates path dependence and hysteresis.

4. Equilibrium Types

Three Qualitative Equilibria

The system admits three qualitatively distinct equilibria:
  • Sustainable Coexistence: C* > Ccrit, Q* > 0, W* > 0. Both IR and IC effectively satisfied; stable ecosystem.
  • Low-Quality Trap: C* ≈ Ccrit, Q* settles low. Creators survive but quality erodes.
  • Collapse: C* → 0, Q* → 0. Creator exodus, quality collapse, AI faces "data drought."

Causal Chain Summary

$$A \uparrow \;\rightarrow\; W \downarrow \;\rightarrow\; \pi_c \downarrow \;\rightarrow\; C \downarrow \;\rightarrow\; Q \downarrow \;\rightarrow\; A \text{ slows}$$
Each mechanism appears exactly once: diversion lives in W, revenue depends on W, creators respond to profitability, quality responds to critical mass, AI growth depends on quality.

Incentive Compatibility Analysis (v3)

In v3, IR and IC are emergent properties of the dynamics, not separate regime switches.

Individual Rationality (IR)

$$\text{IR}: \quad \pi_c = \theta p_L A + p_{ad} W + p_{sub} Q \geq \bar{u}$$
Creators participate when total revenue exceeds outside option. Enforced by entry/exit dynamics: when πc < ū, exit rate exceeds entry rate.

Incentive Compatibility (IC)

$$\text{IC}: \quad p_{sub} \cdot \Delta Q \geq \Delta c$$
Quality investment is incentive compatible when subscription premium exceeds incremental cost. Under [0,1] normalization: psub ≥ Δc.

How Compensation Affects the System

$$\theta \uparrow \;\Rightarrow\; \pi_c \uparrow \text{ (direct)} \;\land\; D \downarrow \;\Rightarrow\; W \uparrow \text{ (traffic)}$$
θ affects two channels: (1) direct licensing revenue θpLA, and (2) diversion D(A,θ) which preserves traffic W. Both reinforce each other.

Policy Thresholds

$$\theta^{IR}: \pi_c = \bar{u} \quad\quad \theta^{IC}: p_{sub} = \Delta c$$
Two critical thresholds: θIR (survival threshold) and θIC (quality threshold). Below θIR, creators exit. Below θIC, quality erodes. Both depend on full parameter vector.
